Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in or reset my password?

Sign in with the email address you used to register or receive your invitation. To reset your password, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. If the reset email is missing, check your spam or junk folder.

How do I accept an invitation or find my properties?

Open the link in your invitation email and continue with Invited in PortföyPro. If you are a property owner and your properties are missing, ask your agency to confirm the invitation email address and check which properties are linked to your account.

How do I manage, cancel or restore a subscription?

Open the subscription screen from Settings on an agency account or Profile on an owner account. Use Apple’s subscription settings to manage your plan or stop its renewal. To restore access, use Restore purchases with the original Apple Account and the relevant PortföyPro account.

How do I request a refund for an App Store purchase?

Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase, then submit the request to Apple. If your subscription shows as active and you are having trouble accessing PortföyPro, email us for help.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account deletion option in Settings → Privacy and security for an agency account or Profile for a property-owner account. If you are the only active agency administrator and the agency has business records, appoint another active administrator first. To stop renewal charges, cancel your Apple subscription separately.
